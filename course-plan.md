# Polish A0–C1 Course Plan for Russian Speakers

Last updated: 2026-11-09 · Living version: [Claude Doc](https://claude.ai/artifact/4bs81aiqShuRSPApgmsDHf)

## Design principles

The course takes a native Russian speaker from A0 to C1 in about 800–900 hours. The B1 state exam is reachable after about 400–465 hours. The syllabus follows the official exam standards (*Standardy wymagań egzaminacyjnych*) and the topic-based layout of *Hurra!!! Po polsku*, *Polski krok po kroku*, *Złoty środek* and *Coś wisi w powietrzu*.

1. **Shorten A1–A2, don't simplify them.** Russian speakers already know cases, aspect and motion verbs. Spend the saved time where Russian interferes.
2. **Every unit has an "Uwaga, rusycyzm!" box** for false friends, calques and differences in which case a verb takes.
3. **Spiral grammar.** A form appears first as a fixed phrase, then gets explained, then is used freely.
4. **Exam task formats from B1 on**, covering all five parts: listening, grammar accuracy, reading, writing, speaking.
5. **Dyslexia-friendly by default** (details in `lesson-format.md`): spoken input comes first, colour-coding stays the same throughout, lessons are split into short blocks, and every text comes with audio.

### What is hard for a Russian speaker

| Area | Problem |
| --- | --- |
| Stress | Fixed on the second-to-last syllable (*uniwerSYtet*), and vowels are never reduced: every *o* is a full [o] |
| Spelling and sounds | sz/cz/rz/ż/dż, ć/ci/ś/si/ź/zi, ó=u, ł=[w], nasal ą/ę and their loss of nasality |
| Copula | Present-tense *jest* is never dropped: *Ona jest lekarką* |
| Formal address | *Pan/Pani/Państwo* take third-person verbs, not *вы* |
| Past tense | Person endings that can move (*byłem*, *Gdzieś był?*) and vowel changes (*mógł/mogła*) |
| Masculine-personal category | *oni/one*, *-li/-ły*, *dwaj/dwie*, *studenci*: a distinction Russian doesn't have |
| Instrumental predicate | Required: *Jestem lekarzem* |
| Genitive after negation | Required: *Nie widzę Anny* |
| Case government | *słuchać* + genitive, *uczyć się* + genitive, *czekać na* + accusative, *do sklepu* |
| Vocative | In active use: *Panie Profesorze, Kasiu* |
| Numerals | *Pięciu studentów przyszło* |
| Short-word order | Where *się, mi, go, by* go in the sentence |
| False friends | *zakaz, owoce, dworzec, czas/godzina/rok, dynia, krzesło, pytać, zawód, uroda, pokój, sklep* |

## Lesson format

How units are split into lessons, the lesson steps, grammar drills and the dyslexia formatting rules are kept in `lesson-format.md`. That file can change without changing this plan.

## Recurring strands

Five strands run through every unit from A1 to B2, because they cause the most errors in the B1 grammar section.

| Strand | What it covers | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which case each verb takes | *słuchać, szukać, potrzebować, używać, bać się, uczyć się* + genitive; *interesować się, zajmować się, opiekować się* + instrumental; *dziękować, pomagać, ufać* + dative; *czekać na, pytać o, dziękować za* + accusative | 5–8 verbs per unit on colour-coded cards, with a model phrase for each (*słucham muzyki*) |
| Masculine genitive *-a* vs *-u* | *stołu, domu, zeszytu* vs *komputera, ołówka*; tendencies by meaning (tools and animals tend to take *-a*; abstract nouns and materials *-u*) | Every new masculine noun is learned with its genitive: *dom, -u* |
| Spelling | *nie* written together or apart (*nie wiem* vs *niedobry*); capitals (*Polak* but *polski, maj*); *ó/u, rz/ż, h/ch*; commas before *że, który, żeby, ale* | A few minutes per lesson, one pattern at a time |
| Particles (from A2) | *no, przecież, chyba, właśnie, akurat, -że* | Heard in the dialogues, then copied in role-plays |
| Pronunciation | Stress, nasal vowels, soft consonant series | The listening step of every lesson |

## Unit 0 and A1 (about 100–120 hours)

**Unit 0, Sounds and spelling (*Wymowa i pisownia*, six sound groups; longer than usual because of dyslexia).** One group of sounds at a time: (1) alphabet and ł; (2) sz/cz/rz/ż/dż; (3) the soft series ś/si, ć/ci, ź/zi, ń/ni; (4) ą/ę; (5) ó/u, h/ch; (6) stress and voicing changes (*chleb* [chlep]). Each group goes hear, say, trace or type, read. Goals: spell your name, read signs, write down an address you hear.

| # | Theme | Grammar | Communicative goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introductions | *być*; *jest* never dropped; yes/no questions with **czy**; *ty* vs *Pan/Pani/Państwo*; **vocative and short first-name forms** (*Kasia → Kasiu, Pan Marek → Panie Marku*); *To jest* + nominative vs *Jestem* + instrumental (as a fixed phrase); nationalities | Greet people, introduce yourself, choose formal or informal address |
| 2 | Family | *mieć* + accusative; possessives; noun gender, including masculine nouns ending in *-a* (*kolega*); adjective agreement; numbers 0–100; *Mam 35 lat* | Talk about family, ages, jobs |
| 3 | Daily routine | Present tense: *-am/-asz*, *-ę/-esz*, *-ę/-isz*, **the *-ować* class** (*pracuję*), **common irregular verbs** (*jeść, wiedzieć, dać, chcieć, móc: mogę/możesz*); *o ósmej*; days of the week | Describe your day |
| 4 | Free time | Accusative singular; *lubić* vs *podobać się*; *nie* + genitive | Say what you like and dislike |
| 5 | Shop and café | Genitive singular, including **masculine *-a/-u* (start of the strand)**; genitive for quantities; prices; *ile?*; *poproszę* | Order and buy things, ask prices |
| 6 | Home and city | Locative singular with **consonant changes** (*stół → na stole, miasto → w mieście, ręka → w ręce*); *w* vs *na*; *jest / nie ma* + genitive; *ten/ta/to* | Describe your flat and where things are |
| 7 | Getting around | *iść/jechać*; *do* + genitive, *na* + accusative; *tramwajem*; basic imperative | Ask for and give directions, buy a ticket |
| 8 | Weather and plans | *musieć, móc, chcieć*; *umieć/potrafić*; *wiedzieć/znać*; months (completely different from Russian); seasons (*zimą, latem*) | Invite someone, accept or decline |
| 9 | Yesterday | Past tense *-łem/-łam/-łeś*; introduce *-li/-ły* | Say what you did |
| 10 | Plans | Future: *będę robić* vs *zrobię*; ordinal numbers and dates; *za tydzień*, *tydzień temu* | Arrange a meeting, talk about plans |

## A2 (about 120–140 hours)

| # | Theme | Grammar | Communicative goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Biography, CV | Aspect in the past tense, focused on how the pairs are formed (prefixes, *-ywać/-iwać*, *brać/wziąć*); **vowel changes in the past tense** (*mógł/mogła, szedł/szła, wziął/wzięła, niósł/niosła*); **surname declension** (*Kowalski/Kowalska/Kowalscy, pani Nowak, państwo Nowakowie*); *w 1990 roku* | Tell your life story, fill in a form |
| 2 | Appearance and character | Full singular adjective declension; **adverbs** (*dobrze, ładnie*) and their comparatives (*lepiej*); comparatives with **niż and od** (*starszy niż brat / od brata*) | Describe and compare people |
| 3 | Clothes shopping | Nominative plural, masculine-personal forms (*studenci, Polacy, profesorowie*); accusative plural; **irregular nouns** (*człowiek/ludzie, dziecko/dzieci, rok/lata, przyjaciel*); plural-only nouns (*spodnie, okulary*) | Shop for clothes, exchange an item |
| 4 | Food and recipes | Genitive plural; full imperative (*zrób, weźcie, chodźmy, niech*) | Give instructions, complain in a restaurant |
| 5 | At the doctor | Dative (*mi, ci, mu*), *jest mi zimno*, *boli mnie*; **impersonal words** (*trzeba, należy, można, wolno, warto*); health fund (NFZ), prescriptions | Describe symptoms, book an appointment |
| 6 | Travel | Locative plural; place prepositions (*na Węgrzech, nad morze/nad morzem*); prefixed motion verbs; **time expressions** (*przez dwa lata, od… do, za pięć ósma, wpół do dziewiątej*) | Plan a trip, book a hotel |
| 7 | Work | Instrumental, including plural (*-ami*, *ludźmi, dziećmi*); polite conditional (*chciałbym, mógłby Pan*); *Państwo* for addressing a group | Make polite requests, handle phone calls |
| 8 | Holidays and traditions | *życzyć* + dative + genitive; verbs with *się*; **swój** and ***siebie/sobie/sobą***; holidays with plural-only names (*urodziny, imieniny*) | Give holiday greetings, describe traditions |
| 9 | Renting a flat | Dative plural; **position verbs** (*stać/stawiać/postawić, leżeć/kłaść/położyć, siedzieć/siadać/usiąść, wisieć/wieszać*) | Call about a listing, arrange furniture |
| 10 | Emails and messages | *który* in all cases; *że* vs *żeby*; **indefinite pronouns** (*ktoś, coś, gdzieś, kiedyś*) and **negative pronouns with double negation** (*nikt, nic, nigdzie*: *Nikogo nie widziałem*); *ani… ani* | Write formal and informal emails |
| 11 | Culture and events | Numerals with nouns (2–4 vs 5+); *dwaj/dwóch/dwie*; collective numerals (*dwoje dzieci, dwoje drzwi*); *oba/obaj/obie*; *kilka/kilku, wielu* | Recommend a film, describe an event |

## B1, the state exam target (about 160–180 hours)

| # | Theme | Grammar | Communicative goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Education | Verbal nouns (*-nie/-cie*); aspect in the imperative (*nie rób!* vs *zrób!*); nouns that don't decline (*muzeum, kakao, menu*) | Compare education systems |
| 2 | Offices and paperwork | Passive with *zostać/być*; impersonal *-no/-to*; impersonal *się*; PESEL, registration of address | Deal with offices, write an application |
| 3 | Health and lifestyle | Real vs hypothetical conditions (*jeśli* vs *gdybym*); *powinieneś*; where *by* goes (*Ja bym…, Gdzie byś…*) | Give advice, talk about hypotheticals |
| 4 | Career | *żeby* with a change of subject (*Chcę, żebyś przyszedł*); **mieć + infinitive** for obligation (*Mam to zrobić do piątku*) | Write a CV and cover letter, make requests |
| 5 | Relationships | Reciprocal *się/sobie*; full vs short pronouns (*jego/go/niego*); order of short words | Describe feelings and relationships |
| 6 | Environment | Linking words for argument; **paired conjunctions** (*albo… albo, zarówno… jak i, nie tylko… ale też*); *mimo że* | Argue a position, agree and disagree |
| 7 | Services and complaints | Indirect questions; reported speech; reported commands (*Powiedział, żebym przyszedł*) | Make a complaint, report what someone said |
| 8 | City and change | Active participles (*-ący*) and passive participles (*-ny/-ty/-ony*) | Describe a city and how it has changed |
| 9 | Poland: history and culture | Full masculine-personal numerals; **fractions and percentages** (*pół, półtora/półtorej, ćwierć, 30 procent*); centuries | Narrate historical facts, read statistics |
| 10 | Media and technology | Adverbial participle *-ąc*; word formation; diminutives | Summarize a text, give a short presentation |

### Exam module (about 20–25 hours)

- Mock tests for each of the five parts, first untimed, then timed.
- **Writing:** formal letter or complaint, informal message, opinion text. Use memorized templates and sentence frames to reduce spelling load.
- **Speaking:** monologue on a topic, picture description, role-play. This is the strongest channel for a dyslexic learner, so practise it most.
- **Grammar accuracy:** written drills on the recurring strands (verb cases, *-a/-u*, *nie* spelling).
- **Reading:** strategies for skimming and finding information in long texts; practise with a reading ruler or line marker.
- Apply for adjusted exam conditions for dyslexia (for example, extra time) well before the registration deadline, and bring documentation. Check the current rules on certyfikatpolski.pl.

## B2 (about 190–210 hours)

| # | Theme | Grammar | Communicative goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Economy and the job market | Past adverbial participle *-wszy/-łszy*; turning verbs into nouns in formal style | Give a formal presentation |
| 2 | Social problems | Past counterfactuals (*gdybym wtedy wiedział…*); *oby, niech* | Discuss controversial issues |
| 3 | Politics and the state | Compound prepositions (*w związku z, ze względu na, w wyniku, na skutek*) | Read and discuss the news |
| 4 | Science | Finer points of aspect: repeated-action *-ywać* (*bywać, czytywać*), aspect after modal verbs and with negation | Explain a process |
| 5 | Law and citizens | Meanings of verb prefixes (*roz-, prze-, od-, na- + się*); **mieć + infinitive for hearsay** (*Miał przyjechać*) | Understand a legal text, file an appeal |
| 6 | Film, theatre and art | Fixed expressions and idioms | Write a review |
| 7 | Register | Colloquial, neutral and formal Polish; forms of address | Switch between registers |
| 8 | Media | Advanced reported speech; discourse markers | Write an argumentative essay (*rozprawka*) |
| 9 | The future | Time clauses with aspect (*zanim, dopóki nie, odkąd*); distributive *po* (*po dwa jabłka*) | Speculate about the future |
| 10 | 20th-century Polish history | Numeral declension (*z pięcioma, o dwustu*) | Narrate events, discuss historical memory |

## C1 (about 210–230 hours)

At C1, audiobooks of literature and podcasts replace much of the reading load. Long texts are read alongside audio.

| # | Theme | Focus | Communicative goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Literature (Szymborska, Miłosz, Lem, Tokarczuk) | Literary and archaic forms: pluperfect *był zrobił*, *byłbym zrobił* | Interpret a text |
| 2 | Writing styles | Official, academic and journalistic Polish; nominal style | Write a summary or synthesis |
| 3 | Persuasion | Rhetoric, irony; regional variants (*na pole* vs *na dwór*) | Analyze and run a debate |
| 4 | Business and specialist Polish | Long participle phrases; business correspondence | Negotiate |
| 5 | Language norms | Russian-origin calques (*póki co, odnośnie (do), na dzień dzisiejszy*); *tę/tą*, *poszedłem*, *wziąć* | Edit your own writing |
| 6 | Humour and pop culture | Advanced particles (*bodajże, -ż*), wordplay, slang; mocking plurals (*chłopy, profesory*), for recognition only | Pick up subtext and nuance |
| 7 | Abstract debate | Hedging (*wydaje się, jak się zdaje*); complex word formation | Argue with nuance |
