# Intent

## Goal

Pass the Polish state certificate exam at **B1** (*egzamin certyfikatowy*, see certyfikatpolski.pl). Longer term: reach C1.

## Learner

- Native Russian speaker, fluent in English; lives in Warsaw (daily exposure available).
- Has dyslexia and dysgraphia. Materials must be **dyslexia-friendly**: listen before reading, colour-coding, low copying load, typing rather than handwriting by default. See `lesson-format.md`.
- Wants written grammar drills (Golitsynsky-style): they worked well when learning English.
- Was taught most of A2 before, but retained it poorly, so there are gaps rather than missing levels.

## Current state (2026-11-09)

- Placement check: A1 75%, A2 71%, B1 30%. Details: `progress/placement-2026-11-09.md`.
- Main gaps: masculine-personal forms, contexts that trigger the genitive, past-tense person endings, consonant changes, irregular present-tense verbs.

## Plan

1. **Repair block (~30 h):** genitive → past endings and *-li/-ły* → masculine-personal plurals and numerals → consonant changes → irregular verbs, *nie* spelling, *od/niż*.
2. **Rest of A2 (~70–80 h).**
3. **B1 units and exam module (~180–205 h).**
4. Retake the placement check after the repair block. Target: above 85% on A1 and A2.

## Schedule

- 3 lessons a week, 60 min each; each unit takes 2–3 lessons.
- Anki daily: 15–20 new cards plus reviews (about 20–35 min).
- Exam readiness estimate: November 2027 – January 2028 at about 6.5 h a week, or August–September 2027 at about 8 h a week.
- Exam sessions are fixed and registration closes early. Apply for adjusted conditions for dyslexia in advance.

## Definition of done

- The B1 certificate is passed.
- Along the way, every unit in the plan has a lesson in `lessons/` and a matching entry in `progress/`.
